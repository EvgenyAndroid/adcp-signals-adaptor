### Takeaway
Evgeny's held positions form one connected argument: HoldCo consolidation (Omnicom-IPG, Publicis-LiveRamp, WPP/InfoSum) is the acquisition of "fuel" (identity substrates) and "steering" (decisioning engines) for agents; the scarce asset in an agentic market is trust/the referee layer, which a CMO or HoldCo must own as "the rules, not the campaigns"; AdCP and AAMP split by ancestry (greenfield vs retrofit), not altitude; and signal containerization plus a governed policy layer is what separates a context economy from a surveillance economy.
