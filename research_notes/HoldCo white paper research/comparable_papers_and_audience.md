# Comparable publications on agentic advertising (2025–Oct 2026) and the HoldCo audience

Research date: 2026-10-05. Scope: white papers, reports, protocol releases and thought-leadership on agentic advertising / AI media buying / agent-to-agent commerce aimed at (or reacted to by) agency holding companies, plus a profile of who inside a HoldCo reads and acts on them.

Method and limitations (important for the writer): the session's web-search budget was exhausted and the egress proxy blocked direct fetches of almost every publisher domain (iab.com, iabtechlab.com, mckinsey.com, bcg.com, accenture.com, forrester.com, gartner.com, warc.com, ebiquity.com, media-sense.com, adexchanger.com, digiday.com, adweek.com, mediapost.com, campaignlive.com, thedrum.com, ana.net, wpp.com, publicisgroupe.com, omc.com, sec.gov, blog.google, thetradedesk.com, agenticadvertising.org, adcontextprotocol.org). Only github.com was fetchable. Consequently: (a) every item below is dated and linked, but lengths/section structures could only be captured where the search-result text or a GitHub page exposed them; (b) statistics are reproduced exactly as the search-result summaries stated them and should be spot-checked against the primary before quotation; (c) where I could not verify a claim I have put it in Gaps rather than guess. Paywall status is flagged per item where known; Adweek, Digiday, AdExchanger, WARC, eMarketer, Forrester and Gartner research are generally paywalled or registration-gated.

---

## Key Question 1: What have the standards bodies, platforms, consultancies, analysts, advisors and the HoldCos themselves published on agentic advertising since 2025 (dates, URLs, headline claims)?

### Takeaway
The field splits into (1) two competing protocol stacks (AdCP under AgenticAdvertising.org, and AAMP under IAB Tech Lab) that publish specs rather than papers; (2) platform launches (The Trade Desk Koa Agents, Google Ask Advisor, Amazon Ads Agent, Meta Andromeda/Business Agent) framed as product news; (3) analyst and consultancy surveys (Forrester/4As, Gartner, McKinsey, BCG-Google, Accenture, Deloitte, IAB, IAB Europe, Digiday/Optable, MediaSense) that quantify adoption but rarely address signals, protocols or auditability; and (4) HoldCo investor-facing narratives (Omnicom Omni/AdCP, Publicis CoreAI/LiveRamp, WPP Open, Dentsu.Connect, Havas Converged.AI) that assert agentic capability without publishing governance detail. No single HoldCo-facing white paper found combines audience signals, protocol choice, governance and receipts/attestations.

### Cited Findings

#### A. Standards bodies and protocol consortia

**AdCP / AgenticAdvertising.org (AAO)**
- AdCP (Ad Context Protocol) launched October 2025 by a supply-side consortium: founding members Scope3, Yahoo, PubMatic, Swivel, Triton Digital and Optable; built on Anthropic's Model Context Protocol; initial release included protocols for Audience Activation, Curation and Media Buy; code went live on GitHub — [MediaPost, 16 Oct 2025](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/409904/supply-side-consortium-unveils-agentic-ad-protocol.html) (registration); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/adcp-ad-context-protocol-agentic-advertising-standard/) (paywalled)
- Each founding member contributed engineering time plus a $10,000 annual budget; supporter list grew past twenty companies including Magnite and Kargo — [tipsheet.ai](https://tipsheet.ai/news/adcp-agentic-ads/)
- AdCP is described as "an open standard that defines how AI agents discover, plan, buy, sell, and measure digital advertising across every channel," published by AgenticAdvertising.org with 116+ member companies; four core modules named: Signals Activation Protocol (audience discovery), Media Buy Protocol, Creative Protocol, and a Curation Protocol scheduled for Q2 2026; supports Google's A2A as a second transport layer — [Fluency](https://www.fluency.inc/blog/what-are-adcp-aamp-agentic-advertising-protocol-standards); [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/agentic-advertising-trade-group-adcp)
- AAO governance: interim leadership Randall Rothenberg (former IAB CEO) and Matthew Egol (ex Booz/PwC); an elected board of 40 directors plus CEO with 10 seats each for Brands, Agencies, Publishers and Technology Providers; first AGM "currently scheduled for April" (2026); interim board also includes Brian O'Kelley and Ebiquity CEO Ruben Schreurs — [tipsheet.ai governance](https://tipsheet.ai/governance/agentic-advertising-org/); [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/agentic-advertising-trade-group-adcp); [INMA](https://www.inma.org/blogs/advertising-initiative/post.cfm/understanding-the-agentic-advertising-moment)
- Marketing Brew reports that "tech and publisher representation is full" on the board while "AAO is looking to add additional board members from brands and agencies" — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/agentic-advertising-trade-group-adcp)
- AAO held an inaugural "Foundry" summit of ~150 senior practitioners — [AgenticAdvertising.org stories](https://agenticadvertising.org/stories); governance page: [agenticadvertising.org/governance](https://agenticadvertising.org/governance); launch note: [adcontextprotocol.org/perspectives/launch-announcement](https://adcontextprotocol.org/perspectives/launch-announcement)
- AdCP GitHub (fetched 2026-10-05): current surfaces are Media Buy, Creative, Signals ("audience and targeting data activation" via `get_signals` and `activate_signal`), Accounts (commercial identity and billing), Governance ("brand suitability and content standards" via `create_content_standards` and `calibrate_content`), Brand (brand.json well-known file), Sponsored Intelligence and Trusted Match; Curation "coming soon"; 5,213 commits on main — [github.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp](https://github.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp)
- AdCP changelog (fetched): latest 3.2.2; 3.2 line adds "signal-quality attestations added to governance checks", "portable runtime signal-quality attestations", "rights-grant attestations with digest-pinned references", "signed authorization binding for monetary commitments", consequential request schemas declaring `x-governed-commitment`, "immutable audit readback for governance outcomes", audit logs that "expose discrepancies, period state, conservative exposure", a versioned "AdCP A2A 1.0 profile extension", and brand-safety do-not-run lists — [AdCP CHANGELOG.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/main/CHANGELOG.md)
- AdCP docs tree includes folders for signals, governance, accounts, media-buy, creative, brand-protocol, sponsored-intelligence, trusted-match, measurement, verification, curation, registry and aao, plus `trust.mdx` and `ai-disclosure.mdx` — [github.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/tree/main/docs](https://github.com/adcontextprotocol/adcp/tree/main/docs)
- Industry reaction piece: "What the Ad Tech Industry Makes of the AdCP Agentic AI Standard" — [ExchangeWire, 23 Oct 2025](https://www.exchangewire.com/blog/2025/10/23/what-the-ad-tech-industry-makes-of-the-adcp-agentic-ai-standard/); "Ad Context Protocol divides advertising industry on agentic AI standards" — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/ad-context-protocol-divides-advertising-industry-on-agentic-ai-standards/); explainer "WTF is AdCP" — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/wtf-ad-context-protocol/) (metered)

**IAB Tech Lab / AAMP**
- LiveRamp donated its User Context Protocol (UCP) to IAB Tech Lab on 3 Nov 2025 "establishing an open standard for how AI agents exchange identity, contextual, and reinforcement signals"; UCP was renamed "Agentic Audiences" and placed in the IAB Tech Lab GitHub — [CDP Institute](https://www.cdpinstitute.org/news/liveramp-donates-user-context-protocol-to-iab-tech-lab-to-set-standards-for-agentic-ai/); [AdExchanger roundup, 5 Nov 2025](https://www.adexchanger.com/daily-news-roundup/wednesday-05112025/)
- IAB Tech Lab released its own agentic framework positioned against AdCP: "IAB Tech Lab, not a fan of AdCP, releases an agentic advertising framework" — [The Current (TTD)](https://www.thecurrent.com/marketing-strategy-iab-tech-lab-adcp-agentic-advertising-framework-ai) (date not captured; appears late 2025)
- IAB Tech Lab formally named the umbrella initiative AAMP (Agentic Advertising Management Protocols) on 26 Feb 2026 "to end market confusion"; AAMP extends AdCOM, OpenDirect, OpenRTB and the Deals API plus LiveRamp's UCP for audience signals, and integrates MCP, A2A, gRPC and Protocol Buffers; three task forces: buyers, sellers, agentic audiences — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/iab-tech-lab-names-its-agentic-ad-initiative-aamp-to-end-market-confusion/); [IAB Canada](https://iabcanada.com/iab-tech-lab-introducing-aamp-a-new-framework-for-agentic-advertising-standards/); [IAB (external link)](https://www.iab.com/external-links/get-aampd-iab-tech-labs-agentic-ad-management-protocol/)
- IAB Tech Lab 2026 roadmap post: "Navigating the Agentic Frontier" — [iabtechlab.com](https://iabtechlab.com/navigating-the-agentic-frontier-the-iab-tech-lab-2026-roadmap/) (blocked; content not read)
- "AAMP 2.0 Release Brings Transaction-ready Buyer and Seller Agent SDKs" — [iabtechlab.com](https://iabtechlab.com/aamp-2-0-release-brings-transaction-ready-buyer-and-seller-agent-sdks/) (date not captured); "Data Standards for the Agentic Age" — [iabtechlab.com](https://iabtechlab.com/data-standards-for-the-agentic-age/)
- AAMP 3.0 with the OpenProposal specification introduced September 2026, "standardizing the RFP to Buy process" — [IAB Tech Lab press release](https://iabtechlab.com/press-releases/iab-tech-lab-introduces-aamp-3-0-with-openproposal/); OpenProposal GitHub (fetched): current draft v3.0-draft-1, public comment period 22 Sep–22 Oct 2026, "Do not build production systems against it expecting stability"; only 3 of 71 fields require negotiation, 68 carry unilateral mutability markers; routes line items to OpenDirect 2.1, Deals API or OpenRTB 2.6 — [github.com/IABTechLab/openproposal](https://github.com/IABTechLab/openproposal)
- AAMP GitHub umbrella (fetched) lists seven repositories: Agentic Direct, Buyer Agent, Seller Agent, OpenProposal, Registry Agent, Agentic Real Time Framework (ARTF), Agentic Audiences; "each repository uses independent semantic versioning"; no audit/receipt mechanisms, A2A or MCP integration mentioned on the umbrella page — [github.com/IABTechLab/aamp](https://github.com/IABTechLab/aamp)
- IAB Tech Lab buyer-agent (fetched): v2.5.0; enables "DSPs, agencies, and advertisers to automate programmatic direct purchases using IAB OpenDirect 2.1"; transports MCP (primary), A2A (JSON-RPC 2.0), REST; "identity-based tiered pricing" revealing seat → agency → advertiser; optional approval gating via IAB Diligence Platform (`iabBuyerAgentApproval` flag); "status-transition audit trail" on buyer orders — [github.com/IABTechLab/buyer-agent](https://github.com/IABTechLab/buyer-agent)
- Agentic Audiences spec (fetched): "an open standard proposed by LiveRamp to enable intelligent agents in advertising and marketing to interoperate through the exchange of signals"; identity, contextual and reinforcement signals encoded as 256–1024-dimension embeddings; consent controls and TTL for consented data; vision includes "agentic attestation" ensuring "confidentiality and integrity of code and information accessed or executed through agents, including provenance and controlled execution environments" — [github.com/IABTechLab/agentic-audiences](https://github.com/IABTechLab/agentic-audiences)

## Key Question 4: What formats and lengths work for this audience, and which 2025–2026 papers can be cited as models?

### Takeaway
The pieces that travelled in 2025–26 were short, numbered and quotable: a 13-slide BCG-Google deck, a five-prediction Forrester note, an 180-respondent survey with a handful of percentages, a blog-length MediaSense essay with one sceptical line about audited results, and spec drafts with 30-day comment windows. Long-form prose white papers were not what circulated; evidence tables, decision gates and checklists were.

### Cited Findings
- BCG-Google: "13-slide research deck" with "2026 research results and findings", ~1,000 respondents, 10 countries — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/holding-company-agencies-rank-last-in-each-of-four-growth-areas-bcg-finds/); [BCG PDF](https://www.bcg.com/assets/2026/the-new-era-of-marketing-partnerships.pdf)
- Forrester "Predictions 2026: Marketing Agencies" is structured as five shifts/predictions with a single headline number (15% job losses) — [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/forrester-predicts-15-agency-job-losses-2026-the-agencies-agents-era-over); [contentgrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/forrester-predictions-2026/)
- Digiday/Optable "State of Agentic Advertising 2026": survey of 180, gated download, organised by investment/readiness/barriers/impact and by audience (agencies, publishers, advertisers) — [Optable](https://www.optable.co/downloads/the-state-of-agentic-advertising)
- MediaSense July 2026 piece: blog-length, single author, mixes one agency claim (80% activation-time cut) with an explicit caveat about self-reported, unaudited pilots — [MediaSense](https://www.media-sense.com/2026/07/14/agentic-ai-in-programmatic-which-way-now/)
- ANA principal-media guidance is framed as "how marketers can safeguard themselves" (checklist/recommendations) — [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/principal-media-buying-how-marketers-can-safeguard-themselves-per-ana/815087/); ANA transparency report is survey + prescription ("use your own, or an independent auditor's") — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/415592/media-transparency-is-still-broken-heres-what-ma.html)
- Madison & Wall's Google-commissioned report is a "long-form publication" PDF with named expert consultations — [Madison & Wall](https://madisonandwall.substack.com/p/the-future-of-planning-is-the-platform)
- OpenProposal v3.0-draft-1 uses a dated public-comment window (22 Sep–22 Oct 2026) and explicit field-level mutability tables (3 negotiable of 71 fields) — [openproposal](https://github.com/IABTechLab/openproposal)
- TTD's agent write-up is a product explainer centred on approval gates and explainability ("asks the marketer to sign-off before executing and explains its recommendations") — [TTD](https://www.thetradedesk.com/press-room/inside-the-trade-desks-claude-powered-campaign-agent)
- Gartner's format is a single-sentence prediction with a date and a percentage (60% by 2028; >40% cancelled by 2027) — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-01-15-gartner-predicts-60-percent-of-brands-will-use-agentic-ai-to-deliver-streamlined-one-to-one-interactions-by-2028)
- A secondary practitioner piece frames the 2026 story as "governed autonomy: the spend caps, approval gates, and audit trails that decide whether you can trust an ad agent with real money" — [Digital Applied (secondary)](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/agentic-advertising-2026-ai-ad-ops-playbook)

### Inferences
- A HoldCo-facing paper on signals and receipts would fit the observed pattern best as: a one-page executive summary with 3–5 numbered claims; a "receipts" appendix (tables of attestations, audit readbacks, activation records, with field names from AdCP 3.2 / Agentic Audiences); a decision framework for buyer-agent vs seller-agent exposure; and a procurement/contract checklist modelled on ANA's safeguard lists. This is an inference from the formats that circulated, not a sourced finding that such a format was "well received".
- Because MediaSense and ANA both attacked self-reported, unaudited agentic claims, a paper that publishes verifiable logs (rather than pilot percentages) would be differentiated.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies reception (downloads, citations) of any 2025–26 paper; "well-received" is unverifiable from captured material.
- Page counts for McKinsey, MediaSense, Accenture, IAB and Forrester documents not captured.

## Key Question 5: What vocabulary and objections recur in HoldCo and client reactions to agentic buying?

### Takeaway
The recurring objections are about opacity and incentive ("black box agentic systems", "agents talking to agents ... we lose sight completely", principal media), platform control ("walled gardens unlikely to open to AAMP", "cut out the middlemen"), unproven claims ("teenage sex phase", "overpromising", "self-reported, few independently audited"), cost ("agencies can't afford to continue to subsidize"), and liability/brand safety ("the brand that benefited ... bears responsibility"; "governed autonomy: spend caps, approval gates, audit trails").

### Inferences
- The phrases "who holds the receipts", "data sovereignty" and "agent liability" did not surface verbatim in HoldCo coverage; the live vocabulary is "black box", "contractual governance", "independent auditor", "human-approved workflow", "agent verification", "shorten the supply chain", "walled garden", "principal media", "overpromising", "subsidize/tokens".
