## Key Question 1: What does each HoldCo call its AI operating system / agentic platform, what does it claim to do in media planning and buying, and what has shipped vs been announced?

### Takeaway
Every HoldCo now markets a named "operating system": Omnicom's next-gen **Omni** (incl. Acxiom Real ID and an "agentic AI framework"), **WPP Open** (with Agent Hub, Open Intelligence and a prototype Buyer Agent for Video), Publicis **CoreAI** (plus Marcel, now extended with Microsoft for "agentic marketing"), **dentsu.Connect** (revamped for agents; formal rollout slated Q4 2026), Havas **Converged.AI** (with the AVA LLM portal and an Akkio-built agentic layer), Stagwell's **The Machine / The Media Machine** (20+ agents, built by Code and Theory and GALE) and Horizon's **HorizonOS / Blu** agentic orchestration layer. Only Omnicom has publicly claimed live, revenue-bearing agent-to-agent media buys for clients (Q1 2026 call); WPP Media's buying agent and Horizon's Blu agents are explicitly prototypes/tests, and Dentsu's and Havas' agentic layers are rollouts in progress.

### Inferences
- Omnicom is the only HoldCo with a public, management-stated claim of revenue-bearing agent-to-agent transactions; all others (WPP, Horizon, Dentsu, Havas) describe prototypes, alphas, pilots or "rollouts" without disclosed buy volumes.
- The platforms converge on the same architecture (identity graph + clean-room + LLM orchestration + agent catalogue), and most depend on Google/Microsoft/Meta models (Dentsu: Gemini/Llama; Publicis: Microsoft; Omnicom and WPP: Google tie-ups) rather than on proprietary models — [The Media Leader](https://uk.themedialeader.com/agency-groups-ai-platforms-explained/); Forrester reports Google overtook OpenAI as agencies' preferred AI partner — [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/google-dethrones-openai-as-agencies-preferred-ai-partner/).

## Key Question 3: What data and identity assets does each HoldCo own or has it bought, and how are they positioned for agents?

### Takeaway
Identity/data is the asset HoldCos are explicitly positioning as the moat for agents: Omnicom now owns Acxiom (Real ID, 2.6bn identities) plus Annalect and Flywheel inside Omni; Publicis owns Epsilon and is buying LiveRamp (~$2.2–2.5bn) explicitly "as an enabler of agentic business transformation"; WPP owns Choreograph and InfoSum (acquired April 2025) powering Open Intelligence; Dentsu's Merkury has been rebranded dentsu.Audiences inside dentsu.Connect; Stagwell relies on Palantir Foundry and The Trade Desk rather than an owned identity graph; Havas/Horizon co-market BluConverged.
